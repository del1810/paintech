EVENT-RELATED GENERAL GUIDELINES AND ADVISORY FOR VISITORS

While visiting PAINTECH Expo 2027, visitors are requested to comply with the following guidelines:

  1. Event Details: This visitor badge is valid for PAINTECH Expo 2027, scheduled from 14–16 May 2027 at Chennai Trade Centre, Chennai, India.
  2. Valid Visitor Badge: Entry is permitted only with a valid visitor badge or e-badge issued or approved by the event organisers. Damaged, altered, or invalid badges may not be accepted.
  3. E-Badge for Entry: Visitors must carry their e-badge received through email or other official communication channels and present it for scanning at the entrance. Entry may be denied without a valid badge.
  4. Non-Transferable Badge: Visitor badges are intended for personal use only and must not be transferred to another individual.
  5. Age Restrictions: Entry for individuals below 18 years of age will be subject to the event organisers' admission policy. Visitors are advised to check the official event guidelines before visiting.
  6. Valid Identification: Visitors may be required to present a valid government-issued photo identity document, such as an Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving licence, or passport, upon request by the organisers or authorised staff. International visitors may be required to present their passports.
  7. Right of Admission: The organisers reserve the right to grant or refuse admission in accordance with the event's applicable entry policies and security requirements.
  8. Security Checks: Visitors may be subject to security screening at the entrance and within the exhibition venue, as required. Visitors are requested to cooperate with the authorised security personnel.
  9. Badge Display: Visitors must wear and display their visitor badges prominently throughout their stay at the exhibition venue.
  10. Appropriate Attire: Visitors are expected to wear appropriate attire in accordance with the venue's rules and the organisers' visitor guidelines.
  11. Cleanliness and Hygiene: Visitors must maintain cleanliness throughout the exhibition venue and use designated waste-disposal facilities. Any penalties for violations will be subject to applicable venue rules and regulations.
  12. Restricted Items: Entry with prohibited or restricted items, including items identified in the venue's security policy, may not be permitted.
  13. Photography and Videography: Photography and videography within the exhibition venue may be restricted. Visitors must obtain the necessary permission from the organisers before capturing or publishing images or videos where permission is required.
  14. Food and Beverages: Visitors must comply with the venue's rules regarding food and beverages inside the exhibition halls.
  15. Personal Belongings: Visitors are responsible for their personal belongings. The organisers shall handle reports of loss or theft in accordance with applicable procedures and law.
  16. Smoking and Prohibited Substances: Smoking, vaping, and the use of prohibited substances are subject to applicable laws and venue regulations. Visitors must comply with all relevant restrictions.
  17. Student Visitors: Student entry, if permitted, will be subject to the organisers' admission policy, applicable age requirements, and valid institutional identification where required.
  18. Compliance with Event Rules: All visitors must comply with the applicable visitor terms and conditions, instructions displayed at the venue, and directions issued by the organisers and authorised event staff. For further information, please visit the official website: www.paintech.in.
  19. Force Majeure: The event schedule, venue, or programme may be affected by circumstances beyond the organisers' reasonable control, including natural disasters, government restrictions, public emergencies, or other unforeseen events. The organisers will communicate any changes through official channels where practicable.
  20. Public Health and Safety: Visitors must comply with applicable public health, safety, and hygiene guidelines issued by the relevant government authorities and venue management.

For more information, please visit the official PAINTECH Expo website: www.paintech.in
