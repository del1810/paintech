About the Expo

A Platform Built for Industry Leaders

PAINTECH Expo is India's dedicated B2B platform for the paint, coatings, raw materials, and surface technology industry. Designed to align with South India's fast-growing industrial and infrastructure sectors, the expo connects manufacturers, suppliers, and technology providers with high-value buyers across the region.

With strong growth in construction, automotive, and industrial manufacturing, Chennai and Tamil Nadu have emerged as key hubs for the coatings industry. PAINTECH Expo brings together industry leaders, R&D experts, manufacturers, and global innovators to shape the future of coatings in India.

This is not just an exhibition — it's the annual convergence point of an entire industry ecosystem, where technology is demonstrated, partnerships are forged, and business strategies are transformed.
