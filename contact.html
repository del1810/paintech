FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is visitor entry free?

Yes — visitor entry to PAINTECH Expo 2027 is complimentary for all registered industry professionals. Pre-registration is required to receive your entry pass and expo guide.

When does exhibitor registration open?

Exhibitor registrations are open now. We recommend early booking to secure your preferred booth location and package. Contact our team at info@paintech.in or +91 95005 32234.

What are the expo timings?

PAINTECH Expo 2027 runs 14–16 May 2027. Expo hours are 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily. The inaugural ceremony is on Day 1 at 9:00 AM.

Are sponsorship opportunities available?

Yes — we offer a range of sponsorship packages including title sponsorship, zone sponsorship, session sponsorship, and digital media partnerships. Contact us for the sponsorship brochure.

Can I attend as part of a group or delegation?

Absolutely. We welcome industry associations, government delegations, and corporate groups. Special facilitation including guided tours and dedicated meeting rooms is available. Contact us for details.

Is media accreditation available?

Yes — journalists, photographers, videographers, and industry publications are welcome. Apply for press accreditation at info@paintech.in with your media credentials.
